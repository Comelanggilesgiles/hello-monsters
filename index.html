A classroom where speaking is the game

Children don't learn to talk by filling in blanks. They learn when they want to tell a monster something.

Play first

Every lesson opens with a game, so shy kids are laughing before they notice they are speaking English.

Talk a lot

Six children at most per class means every child speaks in every lesson, not just the loud ones.

Grow with friends

Kids collect monster friends as they master new words, and parents see each badge in the weekly note.

What happens in the classroom

Bright room, small tables, no desks in rows.

  • Six children, one teacher

    Small groups of the same age, seated in a circle on the monster rug.

  • English only, gestures welcome

    Teachers speak English the whole lesson and use pictures, songs and acting so nobody gets lost.

  • Monster Word Wall

    New words become monsters on the wall. Say the word in a sentence and the monster moves in.

  • Weekly note for parents

    A short message on what your child said this week and a phrase to try at dinner.

Pick your monster level

Placement is free. If your child needs a different level, we move them in the first month at no cost.

Baby Mons

Ages 4 to 5

Sounds, songs and colors. Children answer in single words and short phrases.

Little Mons

Ages 6 to 8

Full sentences about family, food and school. Role-play shops, zoos and parties.

Big Mons

Ages 9 to 12

Show and tell, short debates and story writing. Preparation for school English tests.

Questions parents ask

My child has never studied English. Is that okay?

Yes. Baby Mons and Little Mons start from zero, and the first lessons use lots of pictures, songs and movement.

What happens in the free trial class?

Your child joins a real 40 minute lesson with children of the same age. Afterwards the teacher shares how it went and suggests a level. You can stay and watch.

Can we miss a lesson?

Tell us 24 hours ahead and you can make up the lesson in another class that month.

Do you teach siblings together?

Siblings within two years of each other can share a class. Otherwise each child joins their own level, and the second sibling pays 10 percent less.

How do I pay?

Pay monthly by bank transfer or e-wallet at the start of each month.
